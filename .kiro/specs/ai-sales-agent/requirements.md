# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The AI Sales Agent is an intelligent conversational system integrated into the Seevora website that automatically engages with potential clients, answers their inquiries about Seevora's services, and persuasively guides them toward booking consultations or requesting services. The agent operates as a chat interface that understands client needs, provides relevant information about HR management solutions and audit services, and converts conversations into actionable leads.

## Glossary

- **AI_Sales_Agent**: The conversational AI system that interacts with website visitors to promote Seevora services
- **Chat_Interface**: The user-facing widget or component displaying the conversation between the visitor and the AI_Sales_Agent
- **Conversation_Context**: The accumulated message history and metadata for a single chat session
- **Client_Message**: A message sent by a website visitor to the AI_Sales_Agent
- **Agent_Response**: A message generated by the AI_Sales_Agent in reply to a Client_Message
- **Lead**: A potential customer whose contact information and service interest has been captured
- **Service_Catalog**: The collection of Seevora offerings including HR management, audit services, payroll, recruitment, and compliance solutions
- **Conversation_History**: The complete record of all messages exchanged in a chat session
- **AI_Backend**: The generative AI service that produces Agent_Responses based on prompts and Conversation_Context
- **Response_Generator**: The component that constructs prompts and calls the AI_Backend to generate Agent_Responses
- **Intent_Classifier**: The component that analyzes Client_Messages to determine visitor intent and information needs
- **Lead_Capture_Form**: An interactive form presented by the AI_Sales_Agent to collect visitor contact information

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Chat Interface Availability

**User Story:** As a website visitor, I want to access the chat interface from any page, so that I can get assistance whenever I need it.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Chat_Interface SHALL be visible on all public pages of the Seevora website
2. WHEN a visitor clicks on the chat widget, THE Chat_Interface SHALL expand to display the conversation window within 200ms
3. WHEN the Chat_Interface is expanded, THE Chat_Interface SHALL display a welcome message from the AI_Sales_Agent
4. WHEN a visitor closes the Chat_Interface, THE Chat_Interface SHALL minimize to a compact widget
5. WHILE the Chat_Interface is minimized, THE Chat_Interface SHALL display an indicator if there are unread Agent_Responses

### Requirement 2: Message Exchange

**User Story:** As a website visitor, I want to send messages and receive timely responses, so that I can have a productive conversation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a visitor types a message and submits it, THE Chat_Interface SHALL display the Client_Message in the conversation window
2. WHEN a Client_Message is submitted, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL generate an Agent_Response within 5 seconds
3. WHEN an Agent_Response is generated, THE Chat_Interface SHALL display the Agent_Response in the conversation window
4. WHILE an Agent_Response is being generated, THE Chat_Interface SHALL display a typing indicator
5. THE Chat_Interface SHALL preserve message order with Client_Messages and Agent_Responses displayed chronologically

### Requirement 3: Service Information Provision

**User Story:** As a website visitor, I want accurate information about Seevora services, so that I can understand what solutions are available.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Client_Message requests information about a service in the Service_Catalog, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL provide accurate details about that service in the Agent_Response
2. WHEN a Client_Message asks about pricing, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL explain the pricing structure or direct the visitor to contact sales
3. WHEN a Client_Message asks about implementation timelines, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL provide realistic timeframe estimates
4. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL reference only services that exist in the Service_Catalog
5. WHEN a Client_Message is vague or unclear, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL ask clarifying questions to understand the visitor's needs

### Requirement 4: Persuasive Engagement

**User Story:** As a Seevora business owner, I want the AI agent to persuasively guide visitors toward engagement, so that we convert more visitors into clients.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a visitor expresses a business need, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL highlight relevant Seevora services that address that need
2. WHEN a visitor shows hesitation or concern, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL provide reassuring information and address objections
3. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL suggest booking a consultation at appropriate points in the conversation
4. WHEN a visitor mentions a competitor or alternative solution, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL emphasize Seevora's unique value propositions
5. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL maintain a professional, helpful, and confident tone in all Agent_Responses

### Requirement 5: Lead Capture

**User Story:** As a Seevora sales manager, I want to collect contact information from interested visitors, so that we can follow up with qualified leads.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a visitor expresses strong interest in a service, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL offer to collect contact information
2. WHEN contact information collection is initiated, THE Chat_Interface SHALL present a Lead_Capture_Form
3. THE Lead_Capture_Form SHALL request name, email address, phone number, and service interest
4. WHEN a visitor submits the Lead_Capture_Form, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL validate that email address follows standard email format
5. WHEN Lead_Capture_Form validation succeeds, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL store the Lead information in the database
6. WHEN a Lead is successfully stored, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL confirm receipt and provide next steps to the visitor

### Requirement 6: Conversation Persistence

**User Story:** As a website visitor, I want my conversation to persist during my browsing session, so that I don't lose context when navigating.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a visitor navigates to a different page while a chat session is active, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL preserve the Conversation_History
2. WHEN a visitor returns to a page with an active chat session, THE Chat_Interface SHALL display the existing Conversation_History
3. WHEN a visitor closes their browser tab, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL retain the Conversation_History for 24 hours
4. WHEN a visitor returns to the website within 24 hours, THE Chat_Interface SHALL offer to restore the previous conversation
5. WHEN 24 hours have elapsed since the last Client_Message, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL archive the Conversation_History and start a new session on the visitor's next visit

### Requirement 7: Response Quality and Safety

**User Story:** As a Seevora compliance officer, I want the AI agent to provide safe and appropriate responses, so that we maintain brand reputation and avoid liability.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL decline to answer questions unrelated to Seevora services, business solutions, or HR consulting
2. WHEN a Client_Message contains profanity or abusive language, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL respond professionally and redirect the conversation
3. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL decline to provide legal advice, specific compliance rulings, or guaranteed regulatory interpretations
4. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL acknowledge the limitations of AI when appropriate and offer to connect the visitor with human experts
5. THE Response_Generator SHALL include guardrails that prevent the AI_Backend from generating harmful, discriminatory, or false information

### Requirement 8: Conversation Analytics

**User Story:** As a Seevora marketing analyst, I want to track conversation metrics, so that I can measure the agent's effectiveness.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a chat session begins, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL create a conversation record with session start time and unique identifier
2. WHEN a chat session ends, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL record the session end time and total message count
3. WHEN a Lead is captured, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL associate the Lead with the conversation identifier
4. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL track which services were discussed in each conversation
5. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL record whether a conversation resulted in a Lead capture, consultation booking, or no conversion

### Requirement 9: Integration with Existing Systems

**User Story:** As a Seevora system administrator, I want the AI agent to integrate with our current infrastructure, so that we maintain a unified technology stack.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL store Conversation_History and Lead data in the existing PostgreSQL database
2. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL use the existing Express API routing structure for all endpoints
3. WHEN a Lead is captured, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL create an entry in the same format as the existing audit_requests table or in a new leads table compatible with the current schema
4. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL use the existing environment variable configuration pattern for API keys and settings
5. THE Chat_Interface SHALL be built using React components compatible with the existing Vite-based frontend

### Requirement 10: Administration and Monitoring

**User Story:** As a Seevora administrator, I want to monitor and manage the AI agent, so that I can ensure it operates correctly and improve its performance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE system SHALL provide an admin dashboard page that displays active chat sessions
2. WHEN an administrator accesses the admin dashboard, THE system SHALL display conversation metrics including total conversations, Lead conversion rate, and average conversation length
3. THE system SHALL log all Agent_Responses with timestamps for audit purposes
4. WHEN an administrator views a conversation log, THE system SHALL display the complete Conversation_History with Client_Messages and Agent_Responses
5. WHERE the administrator has appropriate permissions, THE system SHALL allow the administrator to review and flag inappropriate conversations

### Requirement 11: Performance and Scalability

**User Story:** As a Seevora system administrator, I want the AI agent to handle multiple concurrent conversations, so that all visitors receive prompt service.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN 50 concurrent chat sessions are active, THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL generate Agent_Responses within 5 seconds for each session
2. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL handle up to 100 concurrent chat sessions without service degradation
3. WHEN the number of concurrent sessions exceeds capacity, THE Chat_Interface SHALL display a wait message and queue incoming Client_Messages
4. THE Response_Generator SHALL implement request timeout handling to prevent indefinite waits
5. THE AI_Sales_Agent SHALL cache Service_Catalog information to minimize database queries during conversations

### Requirement 12: AI Prompt Engineering and Context Management

**User Story:** As a Seevora content manager, I want to control what the AI agent knows and how it responds, so that we maintain consistent brand messaging.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Response_Generator SHALL use a system prompt that defines the AI_Sales_Agent's role, personality, and knowledge boundaries
2. THE system prompt SHALL include comprehensive information about all services in the Service_Catalog
3. WHEN generating an Agent_Response, THE Response_Generator SHALL include the last 10 messages from the Conversation_History as context
4. THE Response_Generator SHALL format prompts to the AI_Backend with clear instructions to stay focused on Seevora services
5. WHERE the system prompt is updated, THE Response_Generator SHALL use the new prompt for all subsequent conversations without requiring system restart
